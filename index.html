Superbly renovated and presented in pristine condition, 106 Seafield Court is an exceptional three-bedroom home enjoying a prime position on one of the most sought-after roads in this popular estate off Seafield Road. Set within a peaceful cul-de-sac beside a communal green, the property combines stylish, light-filled interiors with a wonderful private garden to create a home of outstanding comfort and appeal.

Inside, the home is beautifully presented, with modern finishes and an abundance of natural light throughout.

The accommodation comprises of a welcoming entrance hallway, bright living room, contemporary kitchen/dining, two generous double bedrooms, a spacious single and a stylish modern family bathroom.

A private driveway provides valuable off-street parking, while a wide side entrance offers direct access to the rear garden and potential to extend subject to planning. A further standout feature is the garden which is mostly lawned with a selection of mature plants and shrubs. A generously proportioned garden room offers a highly versatile space perfectly suited to use as a home office, gym or creative studio.

The location is equally impressive. An excellent selection of shops, schools, churches and leisure facilities is close at hand, while Killiney DART station is just a short walk away and the beautiful shoreline of Killiney Beach can be reached in only four minutes on foot. With convenient access to bus routes, the LUAS, N11 and M50, as well as the vibrant coastal villages of Dún Laoghaire, Dalkey and Sandycove, this is an ideal setting for both everyday convenience and an enviable lifestyle.
